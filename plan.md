## Game Progression

# Match Rewards
There should be no 'metaprogress' in the game. They way you improve is by swapping in actions/players that
work well together to develop and strengthen strategies. No action or player is inherently better than others.
There should be no other rewards than new players/actions - no currency gain or xp. 

# Who you play
Instead of replicating the football league system (that has been done many times), I think a more engaging system
would be based off of the boxing ranking system. The player starts at the bottom of a ranking of say 100 teams and chooses
teams to play against. This works similar to an elo system, where beating higher ranking teams will increase your own 
ranking by more than beating a lower ranking team. Your goal is to reach #1 ranking. This would introduce interesting
strategic decisions for the player to make and let the player choose their own rate of progress depending on their 
judgement of how strong their team is compared to other teams. The player should be able to view any teams players and actions
before challenging them, so they could pick out teams they think they can beat on tactics.
